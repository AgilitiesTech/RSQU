What Mike fixes on the roadside

Repairs that get you rolling again, or safely to a shop.

Flat tires and blowouts

Tire changes on trailers and motorhomes, including dual rears and spares stuck under the frame.

Wheel bearings and hubs

Hubs running hot, smoking bearings and wheels that wobble or won’t turn.

Brakes and breakaway systems

Electric brakes, brake controllers and breakaway switches that lock up or won’t engage.

Lights and trailer wiring

Dead running lights, turn signals and 7-pin plugs, so you can drive legally again.

Hitches, couplers and safety chains

Couplers that won’t latch, bent tongue jacks and damaged chains.

12-volt power and batteries

Dead house batteries, blown fuses and converters that quit.

Slide-outs, jacks and awnings

Slides stuck open, leveling jacks stuck down, and awnings that won’t retract when you need to leave.

Water leaks

Burst lines and leaking fittings, from freeze damage to loose connections.

Roof and storm damage

Wind and hail damage sealed up so you can get home dry.

What to do while you wait

The same advice Mike gives on every roadside call.

  • Get off the road.Pull as far onto the shoulder as you can, turn on your hazards and keep everyone away from traffic, behind a guardrail if there is one.
  • Make yourself seen.Set triangles or flares behind the rig if you carry them. At night, leave your running lights on.
  • Smell propane?Turn off the valve at the tank if you can reach it safely. Don’t flip switches or light anything, and move everyone away from the rig.
  • Smoke, flames or anyone hurt?Get clear and call 911 first. Then call Mike.
  • Send Mike a photo.Snap the problem and the nearest mile marker or exit sign, then text both to 801-232-4995.
