Privacy Policy

Version 2026-11-01 · Effective [EFFECTIVE DATE]

This Privacy Policy explains how [AGILITIES LEGAL NAME] ("Agilities," "we," "us") handles personal information on our field-service platform (the "Platform"). The Platform powers branded websites, request forms, proposals, and payment pages for independent service businesses. When you use one of those sites, you will see the service business's name and brand, not ours.

1. Who is responsible for your information

  • If you are a customer of a service business (for example, you submitted a repair request, signed a proposal, or paid online), the service business is the controller of your information. It decides how your information is used. Agilities acts as the business's service provider (or processor) and handles your information on its behalf and under its instructions. Questions or requests about your information should go to that business first; we will help them respond.
  • If you are a business user (an owner or team member with a Platform account), Agilities is the controller of your account information.

2. Information we collect

From customers of service businesses

  • Request details: name, phone number, location (including GPS coordinates if you choose to share them), vehicle or equipment details, a description of the problem, and any photo you upload.
  • Proposal and signature records: the proposal you viewed, your typed name, your finger-drawn signature image, the date and time you signed, your IP address, and your device and browser information (user agent).
  • Payment information: payment amount, status, and the fact that a Processing Fee applied. Card and bank account numbers are entered into and stored by Stripe, not by Agilities. We receive limited details such as card brand and last four digits.
  • Messages: text message alerts and related delivery records, if the business uses them.

From business users

  • Account details: name, email, phone, business name, branding, and login credentials.
  • Billing and Stripe account connection details, and records of Processing Fees and any subscription charges.
  • Usage and support information, such as actions taken in the dashboard and support conversations.

Automatically

  • Technical data such as IP address, browser type, device information, pages viewed, and timestamps, used for security, fraud prevention, and keeping the Platform working.
  • We use cookies and similar storage needed to log in and keep sessions secure. We do not use third-party advertising cookies.

3. How we use information

  • To deliver requests to the right business and let it respond, quote, and schedule work.
  • To present proposals, record e-signatures, and keep a reliable record of what was agreed.
  • To process payments through Stripe and collect the Processing Fee.
  • To send text alerts about a request when a business enables them.
  • To provide support, operate billing, secure the Platform, prevent fraud, and meet legal, tax, and compliance obligations.
  • To improve the Platform using account and usage information. We do not use customer information to market to customers on our own behalf.

4. Who can see your information

  • The service business you contacted and its logged-in users can see your request, photos, proposals, signatures, and payments.
  • Agilities platform administrators can access business accounts and their records for support, billing, security, and compliance. Photos and signatures are visible only to the business's logged-in users and Agilities platform administrators for support. They are not public.
  • Subprocessors that help us run the Platform (see Section 5).
  • Legal and safety: we may disclose information if required by law, subpoena, or court order, or when needed to protect rights, safety, or property, or to prevent fraud.
  • Business transfers: if Agilities is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, information may transfer as part of that deal, subject to this Policy.

6. We do not sell personal data

Agilities does not sell personal information and does not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not use phone numbers collected on the Platform for marketing.

7. Text messages

When you submit a request, you agree that the service business may call or text you about that request. These are service messages, not marketing. Reply STOP to opt out of texts; message and data rates may apply. Text messages are sent through Twilio.

8. How long we keep information

We keep information while a business's account is active and as long as needed for the purposes above. Signed proposals, signature records, and payment records may be kept longer to resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and meet tax and legal obligations. When a business closes its account, it may request an export of its records; afterward we delete or de-identify information unless we must keep it.

9. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards, including encrypted connections, access controls, and limiting staff access to those who need it. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a breach affects your information, we will notify the affected business and, where required, you.

10. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or get a copy of your personal information, and to not be discriminated against for using those rights. Customers of service businesses should contact the business directly; we support businesses in responding. Business users can contact us at [SUPPORT EMAIL]. We will verify your identity before acting on a request. You can use an authorized agent where the law allows.

11. Children

The Platform is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. Requests and proposals should be submitted by adults.

12. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy. We will post the new version with a new effective date and, for material changes, notify business users by email or in the dashboard before the change takes effect.
