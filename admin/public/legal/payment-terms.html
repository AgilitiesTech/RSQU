  1. When it applies. A Processing Fee applies to every online card or bank (ACH) payment made through the platform. This includes diagnosis deposits (for example, a $129 deposit credited toward the job) and any other deposit, progress, or final payment paid online.

  5. Cash and check. No Processing Fee applies to cash or check payments that you record in the platform. The platform will tell customers that paying by cash or check avoids the Processing Fee.
