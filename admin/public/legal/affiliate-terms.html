1. How the program works

  1. Who can join. The program is by invitation. You must be at least 18 and a U.S. person (a U.S. citizen or resident, or a company, partnership, or other organization formed in the United States) with a U.S. taxpayer identification number. If you join through a HitchPoint business account, that account must be active and in good standing. We decide who joins.
  2. Your share and how long it lasts. Your share (a percentage) and how long each referred business earns you a share (a number of months from that business’s sign-up, or for as long as it uses HitchPoint) are set when you join and shown on your referral page in your dashboard. That page is part of these Affiliate Terms.
  3. Your link and code. You get a referral link and a referral code. Your link works only after you accept these Affiliate Terms.

2. When a business counts as your referral

  1. A business counts as your referral if it creates a new HitchPoint account (a) within 90 days after first opening your link in the same browser, or (b) by entering your code when it signs up. If a business opened more than one affiliate’s link, the first link it opened gets the credit. A code typed at sign-up takes priority over a link.
  2. We may also credit a business to you by hand, for example when you help an owner sign up in person. When we do, we decide the date from which its payments count.
  3. A business that already had a HitchPoint account, or that is already credited to another affiliate, doesn’t count. Our records decide who gets credit. If you think a referral is missing, tell us within 60 days of the sign-up and we will look into it in good faith.

3. What you earn

  1. A share of HitchPoint’s net platform fees actually collected. HitchPoint keeps a platform fee on online card and bank payments a business takes through HitchPoint (see the Payment Processing and Fee Terms). You earn your share of the platform fee HitchPoint actually collects from each business you referred, minus any part of that fee HitchPoint returns or loses, for example on a refund, dispute, chargeback, or ACH return.
  2. What doesn’t count. You earn nothing on Stripe’s processing fees, sales tax, the business’s own revenue, cash or check payments, or any payment made before the business was credited to you or after your share period for that business ends.
  3. Share in effect at payment time. Each payment uses the share and share period in effect when the payment was made. A later change doesn’t change earlier earnings.
  4. Rounding. We calculate each payment’s share in cents and round down.
  5. What you can see. Your referral page shows the names of businesses you referred, their status, and your earnings. You see your earnings from each business by month, not their individual payments. It doesn’t show their payment amounts, revenue, or customers, and you have no right to that information.

4. The 30-day hold and reversals

  1. New earnings are pending for 30 days after the payment they come from, so refunds and disputes can surface. After that they become available to be paid.
  2. If HitchPoint later returns or loses all or part of a platform fee (for example on a refund, dispute, chargeback, or ACH return), or we find that an earning was made in breach of these Affiliate Terms or in error, we reverse the matching earning. A reversal can happen after the hold and after we have paid you. We take reversals out of your pending or available balance, and your balance may go below zero until new earnings cover it.
  3. If your balance is below zero when your participation ends, you agree to repay that amount within 30 days of our written request.

5. Payouts

  1. We pay monthly, after the hold, when your available balance is at least $25.00. A smaller balance carries over to the next month.
  2. We pay by ACH bank transfer to the account you give us, or by check mailed to the address on your W-9. We may use another method if those don’t work. When you choose direct deposit, you authorize us to deposit your payouts into that account by ACH; the authorization stays in effect until you change your payout details or leave the program. If we send a payout in error, you agree to return it, and we may correct it as the ACH rules allow. You must give us accurate payment details and keep them current. When your payout details change, we email you, and we may hold a payout while we confirm the change. We aren’t responsible for payments delayed or lost because your details were wrong.
